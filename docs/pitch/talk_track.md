|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Cover | Vansh | 0:20 | "OnTrack gives every open collections case a next best action, with a person always in charge. AI recommends, rules constrain, humans decide, and outcomes improve the next recommendation." |
| 2 Problem | Vansh | 0:30 | "One customer is written five different ways across five systems. The agent calling about a card can't see the loan in arrears or the pay cut mentioned on last week's call, and 34,872 of 96,000 open cases already carry a hardship flag." |
| 3 Architecture | Vansh | 0:30 | "Four layers on one governed base. A data product factory builds the customer 360, Ask answers questions with SQL shown, a feature store reads hardship out of notes and calls, and the next best action engine recommends within the rules. The teal tags mark where a person decides." |
| 4 Layer 1 | Vansh | 0:40 | "1,015,637 golden customers for 1 million people. Matching precision is 99.8% and recall 99.4%, tested blind on 5,000 known links. We never merge two people with different birthdates. 1,924 uncertain matches go to a data steward. 20 data issues are found and handled, and the contract has 8 rules checked on every build." (Demo: C360 → `VIB-8709`: 4 systems, the open case, its next best action.) |
| 5 Layer 2 | Garv | 0:45 | "Ask in plain English and see the SQL. Certified metrics run as fixed templates; everything else is guarded SQL or a cited policy or call answer. The SQL is select-only, allow-listed columns, no protected or personal data. 21/21 on the dev benchmark, 29/29 on hard unseen questions and 15/15 on customer-360 questions. It refused 31 of 31 unsafe prompts, including prompt injection, and wrongly refused 0 of 14 fair ones." (Demo: one certified question, then the citizenship refusal.) |
| 6 Layer 3 | Kris | 0:30 | "Every note and call is scored for hardship. Recall is 0.97 in notes and 0.99 in calls (1.00 where Claude reads the call), and the customer's own words are kept as evidence. That surfaces 8,883 open cases with hardship the case flag missed." |
| 7 Layer 4 | Anuj | 0:45 | "Rules first: a policy gate with 55 tests. Then models: self-cure AUC 0.83 against 0.72 for the bank's model on the same cases. Then ranking within the day's call capacity, three plain-English reasons, and an agent who accepts, edits or rejects." (Demo: Agent card → `CS-2026-583749`: hardship banner, "My pay got cut in Aug.", hardship referral, why this channel.) |
| 8 Value | Anuj | 0:40 | "Like-with-like back-test: of the cases we'd leave alone, 53% cured on their own, against 45% for the bank. Of the cases we'd act on now, 99.6% really didn't cure. On today's book, that's about $157K more expected payments per round of contacts, valued conservatively. And hardship goes to a person: the bank's engine recommended a collection contact for 71% of them." |
| 9 Governance | Kris | 0:30 | "No protected attribute reaches a feature or a decision; if one ever reaches gold, the build stops: contract rule Q7 checks every gold table on every build. Automated decisions differ by at most 5.4 points across protected groups. Every decision is logged with a reason." |
| 10 Team | Garv | 0:30 | "Three owners, one repo, every pull request tested on the full data before merging. 349 tests, a one-command run, and every change from our design is logged with the reason. Next: retrain from agent decisions and outcomes." |
